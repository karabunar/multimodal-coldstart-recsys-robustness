3 Improvement

A test-time repair. Nothing is trained.

Two additions to the gate (dashed)

VGR (Visual Grounding Regularization) lowers the text weight when image and text disagree. GNB (Gated Neighbour Blend) blends in the text of the 10 visually nearest warm items, for flagged items only.

Applied at evaluation time only. VGR, NB and GNB are my own designs and names.

How GNB repairs one flagged item
warm itemflagged cold item10 nearest by image

The image is trusted to find neighbours. Their text replaces part of the corrupted text. About 0.6 s for all cold items.

The catch · were cold items in training?

Answer

The gains depend on the setting: GNB recovers 85% of the loss only when cold items were in training. Leave them out and the gain falls to +0.0004.
